About the model

Planet positions use real mean orbital motion from the J2000 epoch, so the arrangement matches today’s sky (circular-orbit approximation). Sizes and distances are compressed so everything fits on screen; moon orbits are sped up for readability. Surfaces are generated procedurally in your browser.
